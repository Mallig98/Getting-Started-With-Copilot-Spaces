# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Strategic Sponsor

### Role Summary
Strategic Sponsors are executive-level stakeholders responsible for strategic alignment, budget approval, and organizational impact. They ensure projects deliver value aligned with organizational goals and provide critical support and resources.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and strategic alignment
- Provide budget and resource authorization
- Remove organizational roadblocks and escalations
- Communicate project importance to broader organization
- Review project outcomes and business impact

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with organizational strategy
- Maximize return on investment and business value
- Enable cross-organizational coordination and support
- Measure organizational impact and outcomes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Approves budgets, reviews high-level schedules, receives monthly business impact reports
- **Product Manager**: Aligns project scope and outcomes with organizational priorities
- **Developers**: Provides context on business importance and strategic direction (typically through PM/Project Manager)

### Typical Communication
- Monthly strategic reviews and milestone approvals
- Budget authorization and resource requests
- Executive steering committee participation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects are technical decision-makers who ensure solutions align with enterprise architecture standards, scalability requirements, and technical strategy. They provide technical leadership and governance throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and architecture standards
- Review solution designs for scalability and maintainability
- Ensure alignment with enterprise systems and platforms
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on architectural best practices

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are scalable, secure, and maintainable
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Enable consistent architecture across the organization
- Support long-term system reliability and performance

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Reviews technical designs and PRs, provides architectural guidance
- **Product Managers**: Collaborates on feasibility and technical trade-offs for features
- **Project Managers**: Identifies technical risks and dependencies for project planning
- **Quality Assurance Coordinator**: Defines non-functional requirements and testing strategy

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture documentation
- Code review participation and guidance
- Risk register updates for technical concerns

---

## Quality Assurance Coordinator

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Coordinators own quality standards and testing strategy. They work across the team to ensure features meet quality criteria, validate requirements, and coordinate testing activities throughout the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and testing strategy
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Coordinate testing activities (unit, integration, system, UAT)
- Validate that requirements are met and defects are resolved
- Report on quality metrics and release readiness

### Goals
- Ensure software meets quality and acceptance criteria
- Identify and prevent defects before production
- Minimize production incidents and rework
- Provide transparency on quality throughout development

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides test cases, validates fixes, participates in code reviews for testability
- **Product Managers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria and business requirements
- **Project Managers**: Reports on quality status and testing progress
- **End User Representative**: Coordinates user acceptance testing and validates user-facing functionality

### Typical Communication
- Quality reports and test status updates
- Test case and defect documentation
- UAT coordination and results reporting

---

## End User Representative

### Role Summary
End User Representatives are the voice of the end user, providing feedback on usability, functionality, and business value. They ensure that the product meets real user needs and expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Provide user perspective on requirements and design
- Participate in user acceptance testing (UAT)
- Gather and communicate user feedback
- Validate that solutions solve actual user problems
- Identify usability issues and improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Ensure product meets real user needs and expectations
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction
- Identify gaps between intended and actual usage
- Advocate for user experience improvements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Provides user feedback for prioritization and roadmap planning
- **Developers**: Clarifies user requirements and validates implementations
- **Quality Assurance Coordinator**: Participates in UAT and validates user-facing functionality
- **Project Managers**: Communicates user needs and feedback throughout the project

### Typical Communication
- User feedback and issue reports
- UAT participation and results
- Usability review sessions and feedback documentation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers oversee deployment planning, coordination, and rollout activities. They ensure smooth and controlled transitions from development to production, minimizing risk and disruption.

### Responsibilities
- Create and manage release plans and schedules
- Coordinate deployment activities and rollback procedures
- Manage release communications and stakeholder notifications
- Ensure production readiness and deployment success
- Monitor releases and respond to production issues

### Goals
- Ensure smooth, controlled releases with minimal disruption
- Minimize deployment risk and production incidents
- Maintain clear communication with all stakeholders
- Enable fast, reliable deployments

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Aligns release timing with project milestones and stakeholder expectations
- **Developers**: Coordinates deployment activities and addresses production issues
- **Quality Assurance Coordinator**: Validates release readiness and testing completion
- **Technical Architect**: Ensures deployment meets technical standards and performance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and deployment schedules
- Release notes and stakeholder notifications
- Deployment coordination and post-release monitoring
